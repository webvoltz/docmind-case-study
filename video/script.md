# Walkthrough video: script

A plain-language overview for business leads. It answers one question: can Webvoltz build an AI assistant that people can trust with long, important documents? The technical detail lives in the written docs, not in the video.

Times are from the final voice track of [`docmind.mp4`](docmind.mp4) (1:20, 1920 × 1080). The scenes follow the video's nine chapter cards; see the [storyboard](storyboard.md).

## Voice checklist

- Plain words. "Pieces" instead of chunks, "a second pass" instead of a checker model.
- Problem first, then what we built, then why it can be trusted.
- Webvoltz is the subject doing the work: "we built".
- Short sentences, active voice, no exclamation marks, no hype words.
